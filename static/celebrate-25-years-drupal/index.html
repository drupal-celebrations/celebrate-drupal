Let's celebrate 25 years of Drupal

Drupal turns 25 on 15 January 2026. For 25 years, Drupal has stood as a cornerstone of the open-source movement, evolving from a simple message board into one of the world's most powerful digital experience platforms.

This milestone celebrates a global community that has spent two and a half decades championing web standards, security, and the democratic power of open software.

This is an achievement for everyone who’s ever contributed, shared, or built something with Drupal. 

Thank you for all that you do for the Drupal community.
