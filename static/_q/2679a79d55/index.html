Drupal's 25th Birthday Celebration

Drupal turns 25 on 15 January 2026!

That’s 25 years of open source, innovation, collaboration, and the incredible global community that keeps Drupal thriving. This is a celebration for everyone who’s ever contributed, shared, or built something with Drupal, and that means you!

Let's start dreaming up of own ways to join the fun: meetups, posts, stories, creativity, and more. Whether big or small, technical or creative, planned or spontaneous, every celebration counts. There’s no single way to celebrate, and that’s part of the fun!

Let’s make this a warm, inclusive, community-powered celebration. Join #celebratedrupal channel to get all the latest updates.

Here’s to 25 years of Drupal, and to the people who make it what it is!

Let your creativity shine and win Drupal CMS swag

We want people to get creative as they celebrate the launch of Drupal CMS. 

Take a photo, create a video, paint a picture, knit a sweater, experiment with performance art, bake a cake, form a flash mob, or paint a billboard - celebrate the launch of Drupal CMS in any way you can.

Share your creative contribution on Slack (#drupal-cms-launch) before or during the launch event.

We will pick 5 winners during the livestream who will be rewarded with their very own Drupal CMS swag.
